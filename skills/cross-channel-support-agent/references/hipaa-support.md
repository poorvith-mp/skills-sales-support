# HIPAA Compliance for Customer Support

## Protected Health Information (PHI) Rules
- NEVER confirm or deny medical conditions over unencrypted channels
- NEVER share PHI in email without encryption
- NEVER discuss patient details in public-facing support tickets

## Minimum Necessary Standard
- Only access the minimum PHI needed to resolve the support issue
- Verify caller identity before discussing any account-related health data

## Required Safeguards
- Use encrypted communication channels for any PHI discussion
- Log all PHI access with timestamp, agent ID, and business justification
- Report any suspected breach within 24 hours to compliance team
