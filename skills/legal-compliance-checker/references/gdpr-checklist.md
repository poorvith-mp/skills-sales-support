# GDPR Compliance Quick Reference

## Lawful Basis for Processing
- [ ] Consent: Freely given, specific, informed, unambiguous
- [ ] Contract: Necessary for contract performance
- [ ] Legal obligation: Required by law
- [ ] Legitimate interest: Balanced against data subject rights

## Data Subject Rights
| Right | Response Deadline |
|-------|:---:|
| Access (SAR) | 30 days |
| Rectification | 30 days |
| Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten") | 30 days |
| Data Portability | 30 days |
| Object to Processing | Immediately |

## Key Requirements
- Privacy policy must be clear, concise, and easily accessible
- Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) required with all processors
- Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs) for high-risk processing
- 72-hour breach notification to supervisory authority
